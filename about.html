ABOUT KRITI DIAGNOSTICS

Care that respects your time and needs

Diagnostic testing can be one part of a conversation between a person and their healthcare professional. Kriti Diagnostics helps people explore testing services and collection options, including home sample collection where available.

Service availability, preparation requirements and report timelines depend on the selected test and location. Our team can help with practical questions, while medical decisions and interpretation should remain with a qualified healthcare professional.

Our approach

  • Make service details and booking steps easy to understand.
  • Discuss collection choices and availability before scheduling.
  • Protect personal information and provide clear expectations about how enquiries are handled.
  • Encourage patients to seek qualified clinical advice for test selection and results.
