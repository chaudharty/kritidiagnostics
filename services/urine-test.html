SAMPLE TESTING INFORMATION

About urine testing

Urine tests can include different laboratory examinations, depending on the clinical question. The sample type, collection container and timing may be specified by the clinician or laboratory. Follow the instructions supplied for your requested test.

Collecting a sample

Use only the container and method instructed for your test. Ask the team how soon a sample should be delivered and whether it should be kept under particular conditions. Do not assume a sample can be collected at home for every type of test.

Who should guide test choice?

A qualified healthcare professional can determine whether urine testing is appropriate and explain the results. The information here is general and is not a diagnosis.

Before collecting a sample

Confirm the correct container, collection method and delivery timing for your specific test.
