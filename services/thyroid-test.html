GENERAL TEST INFORMATION

What is a thyroid profile?

A thyroid profile is a group of laboratory measurements selected to help a clinician assess thyroid-related markers. Which markers are requested depends on the clinical question and the person's circumstances. This page does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.

When might a clinician request testing?

A healthcare professional may request thyroid testing during an evaluation or as follow-up when appropriate. Test selection and timing should be discussed with the clinician managing care.

Preparation and collection

Preparation instructions vary. Ask whether fasting or other preparation is needed for your specific request. Share medication questions with the relevant healthcare professional; do not alter medicines on your own. Home collection availability depends on area and scheduling.

Need advice about a test?

A clinician can advise whether thyroid testing is suitable and explain results.
