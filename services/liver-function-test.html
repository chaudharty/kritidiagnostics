Liver Function Test

An LFT is a group of blood measurements related to liver function. A clinician selects and interprets tests based on an individual's situation; this page does not provide diagnosis.

GENERAL INFORMATION

What is an LFT?

A liver function test commonly refers to a panel of measurements from a blood sample. The exact markers included can differ between laboratories and test orders. Results are assessed with clinical history and other findings by a qualified healthcare professional.

When may it be requested?

A clinician may request liver-related blood tests during an evaluation or as part of monitoring when appropriate. The reason and timing depend on the individual clinical question.

Preparation and collection

Preparation can vary. Follow instructions from the requesting clinician or laboratory and ask about fasting if unsure. Do not change medication without professional advice. Contact Kriti Diagnostics to ask about collection options and locality coverage.
