GENERAL TEST INFORMATION

About diabetes-related tests

Blood glucose tests measure the amount of glucose in a blood sample at a particular time or across a defined period, depending on the test. A clinician may consider test results alongside personal history and other clinical information. Results alone are not a diagnosis.

Who may be referred for testing?

A healthcare professional may recommend testing as part of assessment, routine monitoring or follow-up. Do not use an online page to self-diagnose or change a care plan.

Preparation and collection

Some tests may require fasting, while others do not. Follow the specific instructions from the ordering clinician. Continue or change medication only on advice from the relevant healthcare professional. Ask our team about collection options in [CITY] and nearby areas.
