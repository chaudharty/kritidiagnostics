ABOUT THE TEST

What does a lipid profile measure?

A lipid profile is a blood test that measures specified lipids. The exact components included depend on the test ordered. Results are interpreted by a qualified healthcare professional with other relevant information; this page cannot assess personal health risk.

Preparation guidance

Fasting instructions can vary with the test and clinical purpose. Do not assume fasting is needed or unnecessary. Follow the instructions from the clinician or laboratory. Ask about medication questions rather than changing medicines independently.
