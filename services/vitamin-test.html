GENERAL INFORMATION

About vitamin profile testing

A vitamin profile may include one or more laboratory measurements. The tests offered and markers measured vary. A clinician may request a test when it is relevant to a person's health assessment; a result should be reviewed in clinical context.

Preparation and sample collection

Preparation requirements vary with the test requested. Confirm any fasting or timing instructions before your appointment. Discuss medicines and supplements with your healthcare professional; do not change them on your own. Home collection may be available depending on locality and scheduling.

Choosing a test

Symptoms alone do not determine which test is needed. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, test selection and interpretation.
