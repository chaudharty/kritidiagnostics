UNDERSTAND THE PACKAGE

What are health packages?

A health package is a combined set of relevant laboratory tests arranged into a single screening option. Package contents, preparation and availability can vary. The right package depends on your health goals, age, symptoms and medical history.

How to choose the right package

  • Review the exact names of the tests included.
  • Check whether fasting or sample preparation is required.
  • Ask whether home collection is available in your area.
  • Discuss suitability with a qualified healthcare professional.

Preparation and results

Preparation varies by test. Follow the instructions provided for your selected package and ask healthcare professionals if the proposed screening is appropriate for your situation.
