CHECKUP INFORMATION

What does a full body checkup include?

There is no single universal set of tests called a full body checkup. Packages can differ in their included panels and intended use. Review the exact list before booking and ask whether any test requires special preparation.

Who may consider screening?

Screening decisions depend on factors such as age, personal history and clinical guidance. A package should not be used as a substitute for an assessment when someone has symptoms or a health concern. A clinician can help decide which tests are relevant.

Preparation and follow-up

Preparation instructions depend on the tests in the package. Follow the directions provided and do not change medication without professional advice. Discuss results with a qualified healthcare professional who can interpret them in context.
