A MORE CONVENIENT COLLECTION OPTION

What is home blood collection?

Home blood collection is an appointment option where a collection professional visits a requested address to collect a sample for a booked test. Whether this service is available depends on the location, test and appointment schedule. Kriti Diagnostics can confirm current availability for [CITY] and nearby areas.

Who may consider a home visit?

People who prefer collection at home may ask whether this option fits their circumstances. A home visit is a collection method, not a substitute for clinical advice. A clinician should guide test selection and interpret results.

What to expect

  • Contact the team with your location and requested test.
  • Confirm service coverage, appointment time and any preparation instructions.
  • Have the collection area ready and follow the instructions provided.
  • Ask when and how the report is expected to be available.

Preparing for a collection

Requirements depend on the test. Some tests may require fasting and some do not. Follow test-specific instructions. Do not stop or change medications unless the relevant healthcare professional advises you to do so.

Availability varies

Home visit service area and appointment slots must be confirmed before a booking is considered final.
