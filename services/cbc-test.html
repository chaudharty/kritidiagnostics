Complete Blood Count (CBC) Test

A CBC measures several types of cells and related parameters in a blood sample. It is one of many tests a healthcare professional may request as part of an evaluation.

GENERAL TEST INFORMATION

What does a CBC measure?

A Complete Blood Count commonly reports measurements related to red blood cells, white blood cells and platelets. The exact report format may vary. A CBC result is interpreted alongside a person's history and other findings and does not diagnose every condition by itself.

Why might a clinician request a CBC?

A qualified healthcare professional may request a CBC for routine monitoring, an assessment or follow-up when clinically appropriate. The reason and meaning should be discussed with the clinician who requested the test.

Preparation and collection

Many routine CBC requests do not require special preparation, but instructions can differ if other tests are ordered at the same time. Follow your specific instructions. Ask about collection availability in [CITY] and nearby areas.
