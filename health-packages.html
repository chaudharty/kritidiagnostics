HEALTH PACKAGE FAQS

Health Package FAQs

A health package combines a group of selected screening tests. The exact tests vary by package and should be reviewed before booking.

Your healthcare professional can help decide which profile best fits your needs, age and current health goals.

Some tests require fasting and some do not. Please follow the preparation instructions for the selected package.

Home sample collection availability depends on your area and the selected package. Please ask the team to confirm availability.

Customization may be available depending on service availability and the tests requested. Contact the team to discuss your requirements.

Use the Book Package button on the page or call the support team. The static website form does not send a booking to a server.

Report timelines depend on the selected tests and laboratory processing. Ask the team for the expected timeframe when you book.
